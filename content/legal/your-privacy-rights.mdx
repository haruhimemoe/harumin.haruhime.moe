## Getting or deleting your data

Email us with your Discord id and we'll send or delete what harumin keeps about you: the servers you were seen in, settings you saved and `/track` entries you added. Server managers can change their server's settings on the dashboard at any time. Your haruhime account (the osu! and Discord links and sessions) is deleted on [haruhime.moe/account](https://www.haruhime.moe/account).
