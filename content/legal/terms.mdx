## Use it fairly

- Don't use harumin to spam, scrape osu! in bulk, or get around osu!'s or Discord's rules.
- Don't try to break it, overload it, or reach data that isn't yours.

harumin may stop answering a person or a server that does these things.

## The dashboard

Anyone with an osu! account can sign in, with osu!, on [haruhime.moe](https://www.haruhime.moe), the account every haruhime tool shares. The dashboard only shows servers where harumin is a member and you have Manage Server, and only changes that server's harumin settings.
